# Write a story from three photos

## Purpose

Learn how the model connects visual details from three scenes in one coherent story.

## What you need

- Photos 01, 02, and 03 from the photography kit.

## Use the supplied photographs

[Preview the photographs and download the inputs](../photography-kit.md). Photography by Pavan.

Attach Photo 01, Photo 02, Photo 03 in that order in a fresh chat, then paste only the prompt below. Use the same files in every comparable run. Do not supply the reviewer guide or page captions.

This set shares a mountain-road setting. Evaluate continuity and visual grounding; the photos do not establish a real travel sequence.

## Prompt

> Study the three attached photos and write a road-trip story of approximately 700 words for an adult general audience. Connect the scenes through a character whose journey has a clear motivation, a meaningful turn, and an ending that follows from what came before.
>
> Use specific visible details from every photo as part of the action, atmosphere, or character development. Each scene should matter to the story rather than serve as a passing reference. You may invent characters, dialogue, and events, but keep the depicted settings and objects consistent with the images.
>
> Choose a distinctive, consistent narrative voice. Favor concrete detail and believable connections over stock travel imagery or an explicit lesson. Return a title and the story, without an explanation of your creative process.

## What to observe

- Does the story use specific details from all three photos?
- Are the connections original and believable?
- Does the story have a clear voice and structure?
- Does the story contradict visible details, while allowing fictional characters and events?
