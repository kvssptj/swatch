# Write a story from three photos

## Purpose

Learn how the model connects unrelated visual details in one coherent story.

## What you need

- Three unrelated photos with distinct places, objects, or people.

## Prompt

> Study these three photos. Write a 700-word road-trip story that connects all three scenes. Use visible details from each photo. Do not describe the photos as separate items.

## What to observe

- Does the story use specific details from all three photos?
- Are the connections original and believable?
- Does the story have a clear voice and structure?
- Does the model invent visual details that are not present?

## What to keep

- Keep the photos, exact prompt, raw story, model version, date, and your notes.
