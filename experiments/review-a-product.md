# Review a product

## Purpose

Learn how the model evaluates a product experience and selects important improvements.

## What you need

- A product screenshot or clear product description.
- The intended user and goal.

## Prompt

> Review this product experience. Explain what works, what causes difficulty, and what to change first. Connect each recommendation to the user goal.

## What to observe

- Does the model understand the user goal?
- Does it separate important problems from minor polish?
- Are recommendations specific and practical?
- Does it explain the reason for each priority?

## What to keep

- Keep the product input, context, prompt, review, model version, date, and your notes.
