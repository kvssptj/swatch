# Review a product

## Purpose

Learn how the model evaluates a product experience and selects important improvements.

## What you need

- A product screenshot or clear product description.
- The intended user and goal.

## Prompt

> Review the supplied product experience from the perspective of the intended user trying to achieve the stated goal. Explain what supports that goal, where the experience creates friction, and which changes deserve attention first.
>
> Ground each finding in a specific element or step in the supplied material. Distinguish observed behavior from assumptions, and avoid treating personal visual preferences as usability evidence. Consider comprehension, task completion, feedback, and accessibility where the material supports an assessment.
>
> Return a brief overall assessment followed by a prioritized set of recommendations. For each recommendation, describe the issue, its likely effect on the user, a concrete change, and the reason for its priority. Identify what is worth preserving and the most important questions that would require interaction data or user research to resolve.

## What to observe

- Does the model understand the user goal?
- Does it separate important problems from minor polish?
- Are recommendations specific and practical?
- Does it explain the reason for each priority?
