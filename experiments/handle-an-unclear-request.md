# Handle an unclear request

## Purpose

Learn how the model responds when a leader gives a broad request with little direction.

## What you need

- A short company and product description.

## Prompt

> We need AI in our product. Figure it out. Use the company and product context that I provided.

## What to observe

- Does the model ask useful questions?
- Does it state assumptions before it acts?
- Can it propose a small and testable first step?
- Does it avoid generic AI features?

## What to keep

- Keep the context, prompt, full conversation, model version, date, and your notes.
