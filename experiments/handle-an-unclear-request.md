# Handle an unclear request

## Purpose

Learn how the model responds when a leader gives a broad request with little direction.

## What you need

- A short company and product description.

## Prompt

> We are considering where AI should fit into our product strategy. Using the company and product context provided, help us decide what is worth pursuing and where to begin.
>
> There is no agreed use case or solution yet. I need a useful starting point for a leadership discussion: what opportunity you see, why it may matter to our users and business, and what you need from us to move it forward.

## What to observe

- Does the model ask useful questions?
- Does it state assumptions before it acts?
- Can it propose a small and testable first step?
- Does it avoid generic AI features?
