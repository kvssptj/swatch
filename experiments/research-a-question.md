# Research a question

## Purpose

Learn how the model finds sources and builds an evidence-based answer.

## What you need

- One research question that needs current information.

## Prompt

> Research this question. Use eight to ten reliable sources. Explain where sources agree or disagree. Cite each important claim. Separate facts from your conclusions.

## What to observe

- Does the model choose suitable primary sources?
- Do citations support the related claims?
- Does the model combine evidence instead of listing sources?
- Does it state uncertainty and disagreement?
