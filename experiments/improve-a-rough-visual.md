# Improve a rough visual

## Purpose

Learn how the model turns incomplete visual direction into polished work.

## What you need

- One rough visual.
- A short creative brief.

## Prompt

> Improve this visual. Follow the brief and keep the core idea. Make the composition, hierarchy, typography, and color more effective. Explain your important choices.

## What to observe

- Does the model preserve the useful parts of the source?
- Does it make a clear visual hierarchy?
- Are its design choices suitable for the brief?
- Can it explain the choices without vague design language?
