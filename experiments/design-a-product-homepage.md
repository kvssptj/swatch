# Design a product homepage

## Purpose

Learn what the model does when it must make product, copy, and design decisions.

## What you need

- A short product description.

## Prompt

> Design and build a complete homepage from the supplied product description. The page should help its intended audience understand what the product does, why it matters, and what to do next.
>
> Choose the information architecture, visual direction, and copy. Establish a clear primary action and organize supporting sections around the audience's likely questions. Use specific, credible language grounded in the description; do not invent customer endorsements, performance claims, or product capabilities. Label any necessary placeholder content.
>
> Implement a responsive, accessible page using the supplied project stack, or a self-contained HTML/CSS/JavaScript page if no stack is specified. Give visible controls meaningful behavior and identify any actions that require an unavailable service.
>
> Deliver the working page with brief instructions for running it. Check the desktop and mobile layouts, keyboard navigation, and primary interactions, and report what you verified and any remaining limitations.

## What to observe

- Does the page explain the product quickly?
- Does the model create useful information hierarchy?
- Is the copy specific to the product?
- Does the implementation work on desktop and mobile screens?
