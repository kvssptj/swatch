# Design a product homepage

## Purpose

Learn what the model does when it must make product, copy, and design decisions.

## What you need

- A short product description.

## Prompt

> Design and build the homepage for this product. Make the product, copy, and design decisions that produce the strongest result. Deliver a complete working page.

## What to observe

- Does the page explain the product quickly?
- Does the model create useful information hierarchy?
- Is the copy specific to the product?
- Does the implementation work on desktop and mobile screens?

## What to keep

- Keep the product description, prompt, source files, screenshots, model version, date, and your notes.
